Exposure is not the same as replaceability.

An occupation can have most of its tasks touched by AI and still be hard to displace, because what remains is the part with no objective outcome to learn from. Search a profession for its exposed share, then look at how that share is distributed across the levels of an organisation.

    Why the top is harder to replace

    Seniority concentrates exactly the work that automation handles worst.

    • Decisions with no objective outcome measure to learn from, made under genuine ambiguity
    • Accountability that has to attach to a person, not a system
    • Relationship capital and institutional memory held outside any document
    • Tasks that are rare, high-stakes and therefore thin on training data

    Why firms invest more in them anyway

    The investment case runs on what the marginal hour is worth, not on how exposed it is.

    • A high wage bill means a small time saving returns more per person
    • Complementary tools raise the value of judgment rather than substituting for it
    • Errors at the top are expensive, so better information pays for itself quickly
    • At the bottom, the same exposure resolves as substitution, and the saving shows up as fewer roles rather than better ones
